Ringy to Gmail

Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+E shows the lead and a template picker. Alt+Shift+1 and Alt+Shift+2 open template 1 or 2 straight away. Template 3 has no key until you set one; change shortcuts here.

Templates

Paste the subject and body of your Gmail templates here (Gmail doesn't let other tools read its templates). You can use {first_name}, {last_name}, {full_name} and {email} and they'll be filled in from the Ringy lead. Your Gmail signature is still added by Gmail.
